Student Lab · Berghs · Bravida

Bravida Student Lab

A strategic communication concept developed during Student Lab at Berghs.

01The challenge

During a Student Lab at Berghs, I worked with a team on a strategic communication challenge for Bravida. The brief was to explore how the company’s broad role in Nordic society could be made more relevant and understandable to more people.

02The work

The work covered audience analysis, brand positioning, concept development and examples of how a long-term idea could work across markets and communication areas.

  • Audience analysis
  • Brand positioning
  • Concept development
  • Scalability across markets and communication areas

03The result

The result was a strategically grounded communication concept that received very positive feedback from the client.

04About the case

Out of consideration for the client, I am not sharing the solution itself. The project shows our ability to turn a complex brand challenge into a clear, relevant and well-received idea.
