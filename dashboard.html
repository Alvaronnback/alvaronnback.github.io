Google Merchandise Store

A dashboard visualising and summarising the Google Merchandise Store audience using Google Analytics data.

The dashboard makes Analytics data easier to navigate and provides a clearer picture of the people visiting Google Merchandise Store. It brings audience data together in an accessible overview.
