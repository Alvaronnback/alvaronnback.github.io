Berghs · Kungliga Operan · Communication & UX

Kungliga Operan

A strategic project exploring how communication, data and UX can bridge the gap between curiosity and a first visit to the opera.

01The challenge

During a project at Berghs, our team was asked to help Kungliga Operan reach new audiences and bridge the gap between curiosity and a first visit to the opera.

02Research & insights

Through interviews, surveys, web analytics and digital customer journey mapping, we identified barriers in both communication and the path to buying tickets.

  • Interviews
  • Surveys
  • Web analytics
  • Digital customer journey mapping

03From insight to concept

The insights were developed into a coherent concept combining brand communication, activation and a more user-friendly digital experience. Research and data informed the creative and strategic work on the digital customer journey and UX.

  1. Research
  2. Insight
  3. Concept
  4. Customer journey
  5. Action

04The result

The proposal was received very positively by the client, who particularly appreciated the combination of creativity and practical feasibility.

05About the case

The project shows how communication, data and UX can work together throughout the customer journey, from initial interest to action.
